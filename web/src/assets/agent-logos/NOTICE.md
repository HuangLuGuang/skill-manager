# Product marks

These files identify third-party products. Brand marks remain the property of their owners.

- Claude, Cursor, OpenCode, OpenClaw, Trae, CodeBuddy, Gemini and Hermes SVGs are unchanged copies of the existing desktop assets, sourced from Lobe Icons `@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.0 (MIT). See `LICENSE-lobe.txt` and https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons.
- `teleagent.png` is an unchanged copy of the desktop asset extracted from the installed official TeleAgent application. Copyright remains with its owner.
- `doubaowork.png`, `qwenwork.png` and `zcode.png` are extracted from the installed official DoubaoWork, QwenWorkCN and ZCode application icons, resized to 128px. Copyright remains with their respective owners.
- `workbuddy.png` is an unchanged copy of the desktop asset extracted from the installed official WorkBuddy application. Copyright Tencent.
- `codex.png` is the unmodified `icon-codex-light.png` supplied in the installed official ChatGPT.app Resources directory. Copyright OpenAI.
- `qoder.png` is the unmodified `application-icons/qoder-light.png` supplied in the installed official Qoder.app Resources directory. Copyright its owner.

Qoder entries share the Qoder brand mark; TraeWork uses the Trae brand mark. Entry labels and capability states remain separate. Assets are bundled locally and do not make requests to external image hosts.
